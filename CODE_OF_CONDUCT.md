# Code of Conduct

IceStack is a place to learn how to make engineering decisions, and that only works when people feel safe asking questions they think are basic, admitting mistakes they made in production, and disagreeing about trade-offs.

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

## Expected

- Be welcoming to beginners and to people writing in their second language. English and Khmer are equally welcome.
- Critique ideas and code, never the person. "This retry has no backoff" is useful; "you clearly don't understand retries" is not.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Credit other people's work and experience.
- Accept a maintainer's decision on a pull request gracefully, even when you disagree.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes or language about anyone's identity, background, or level of experience.
- Publishing someone's private information, including company or customer data from a production story, without permission.
- Spam, self-promotion, and vendor pitches disguised as contributions.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews — and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Email **veasna3d@gmail.com** with what happened, where, and any links or screenshots. Reports are kept confidential. You will get a reply within a week.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban a contributor, following the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
