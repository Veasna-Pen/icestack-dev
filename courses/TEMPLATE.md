## The problem

A situation the reader will meet at work, in two or three sentences. Use real numbers: "the product page takes 900 ms and the database is at 85% CPU". End with the question this lesson answers.

## The idea

The one concept that solves it, in plain words. Define every new term the first time you use it. A short list or a small table helps more than a long paragraph.
