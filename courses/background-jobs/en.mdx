A sign-up request that saves a user, sends a welcome email, resizes an avatar and notifies a CRM can take seconds, and fails whenever any of those services has a bad minute. Most of that work doesn't need to happen before the user sees "Welcome".

This course shows how to move it out of the way. You start by deciding **what** to move, then hand it to a **queue** and **workers**, then handle jobs that keep failing with a **dead-letter queue**, and finish with the question every queue eventually asks: what happens when work arrives faster than you can do it?

The examples use Node.js with BullMQ on Redis, but the ideas are the same with Sidekiq, Celery, Laravel queues, RabbitMQ or SQS.
