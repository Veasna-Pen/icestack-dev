Sooner or later every service you call will be slow or unavailable: a payment provider, a shipping API, another team's service. What matters is what **your** app does then. Does it wait forever? Charge a customer twice? Pull the rest of your system down with it?

This course builds four habits, each on top of the last. **Timeouts** stop you waiting forever. **Retries** recover from brief failures. **Idempotency** makes those retries safe for writes like payments. **Circuit breakers** stop you making a bad situation worse when a service is truly down.

The examples use TypeScript and HTTP, but every idea works the same in any language and with any protocol.
